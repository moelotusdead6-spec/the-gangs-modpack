The End biome lookup in VanillaCompat is patched to fall back to the selected
biome when the End search tree has not been initialized. Complete modified
source, based on upstream tag 1.0.1-beta.1, is available at
sources/biolith-fabric-1.0.1-beta.1-endfix-source.zip.

The hosted-server endfix-concurrency variant additionally patches
DimensionBiomePlacement to rebuild replacement and sub-biome lists privately
and publish immutable snapshots with volatile visibility. Writers are serialized;
normal biome reads remain lock-free. Biome rates and saved ordering are unchanged.
The existing VanillaCompat End fix and all unrelated jar entries are retained.
